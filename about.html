About & privacy

What ScholarPath is, who made it, and what happens to your papers. Last updated 13 October 2026.

What ScholarPath is

ScholarPath is a free research companion for master's students and early researchers. It walks you through four parts: writing a paper, critiquing a paper (reading it in simple English or Arabic, explaining its figures, writing the critique and tracing your evidence), presenting it, and verifying and defending your work before you submit it.

It was designed by Dima Alkharraz, an English teacher in Abu Dhabi and a master's student in Artificial Intelligence, first for the Advanced Research Methods course (0106112) at Al Ain University. ScholarPath is an independent project. It isn't run by Al Ain University or by Anthropic.

Your privacy in one paragraph

No account, no tracking, no uploads. ScholarPath has no login, no analytics and no advertising cookies. The papers you add and everything you write stay in your own browser, on your own device. ScholarPath doesn't send them to us or to anyone else.

لا حساب، ولا تتبّع، ولا رفع للملفات. لا يتطلب ScholarPath تسجيل دخول، ولا يستخدم أدوات تحليل أو ملفات تعريف إعلانية. تبقى الأوراق البحثية التي تضيفها وكل ما تكتبه داخل متصفحك على جهازك فقط، ولا يرسلها الموقع إلينا أو إلى أي جهة أخرى.

Where your work is kept

  • Your papers, notes, critiques and settings are saved in this browser's storage (localStorage), and figure pictures in its IndexedDB. They are only on this device and in this browser.
  • Another browser or device won't see them. Clearing your browsing data, or using a private window, removes them.
  • To keep a copy or move your work, use My Progress → Back up, then restore the file where you need it. The backup file stays with you.

How the Claude features work

Some steps can use Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic: simple-English versions, Arabic translations, Ask Claude, figure explanations, slide outlines, verification and professor questions.

  • On this website, they work by copy and paste. ScholarPath shows you the exact prompt, you paste it into your own Claude chat yourself, then paste Claude's reply back. ScholarPath never sends anything to Claude on its own, so you always see what is shared. Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send in your Claude chat.
  • On the claude.ai version of ScholarPath, the same features run directly with your own Claude account.
  • Everything Claude writes is marked as Claude's. Check it against the paper before you rely on it: ScholarPath never treats Claude's text as a source.

Outside services the page uses

  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, as it does on most websites. Google can see your IP address but not your work.
  • Read aloud uses your device's own voices. Some browsers' natural voices (for example in Microsoft Edge) are made online by the browser's maker.
  • Voice typing in the mock professor uses your browser's speech recognition. In Google Chrome, that sends your speech to Google to turn it into text. Typing your answer instead sends nothing.
  • The PDF reader and PowerPoint maker are included with ScholarPath. Only if they fail to load does the browser fetch the same open-source code from cdnjs or jsDelivr.
  • Search links to IEEE Xplore, Google Scholar, Scopus and the other databases open those sites in a new tab. What you do there follows their own privacy policies.

Using ScholarPath well

  • ScholarPath helps you study and organise your own work. It doesn't write your assignment for you, and it doesn't replace your course's rules on academic integrity or AI use. Follow your university's policy and say when you used AI.
  • ScholarPath never invents findings, numbers, references or page numbers. Where something is unknown, it leaves a clearly labelled gap for you to fill from the paper.
  • Example and demo content is always labelled as an example. It isn't a real study.
